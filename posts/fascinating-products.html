List of products being built that I find super interesting + am following.

01

Augmental MouthPad

A custom-fit retainer that lets you control your phone, tablet, or computer with tongue movements. Designed for people with limited hand mobility.

02

Eight Sleep Pod

A mattress cover that heats and cools each side of the bed independently, from 55°F to 110°F. Tracks sleep biometrics and auto-adjusts temperature throughout the night.

03

Mill Food Recycler

A kitchen bin that dries and grinds food scraps overnight, shrinking them by ~80%. The grounds can be sent back to Mill to be turned into chicken feed.

04

Neko Health

A full-body health scan that uses sensors instead of blood draws to screen for skin cancer, cardiovascular issues, and metabolic conditions in about 15 minutes. Co-founded by the Spotify CEO.

05

Spicy Dan

A Brooklyn-based jewelry brand known for colorful carabiner charm necklaces, bracelets, and enamel dog collars. Started in 2023 and built mostly through word of mouth and social.
